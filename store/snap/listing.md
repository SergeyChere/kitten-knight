**Description:**
The Dusk Lord stole the dawn and hid it behind magic gates that open only while sunlight falls on them. Join the Masked Kitten, the last knight of the Order of the Sun, and run before the sunset across eight magical lands!

* Catch sparks, hold the sun: golden sparks keep the sun in the sky. Stop catching them and the sun starts to set.
* Arrow keys or gamepad: change lanes, jump, slide and strike with your sword.
* 8 magical lands: Bridge of Mists, Golden Dunes, Snowy Forest, Sakura Garden, Fire Mountains, Crystal Caves, Sky Path and Pumpkin Thicket.
* Dusk Guardians: dodge their attacks and strike back in sword fights at the gates.
* Crafty foes: swooping crows, ghosts, hopping slimes, shield goblins, rolling boulders and more.
* Pick your hero: kitten, puppy, hamster, bunny, fox, panda, baby elephant or giraffe, with masks, helmets, capes, outfits and weapons.
* Upgrades and achievements. No ads, no accounts, plays offline.

Controls: ← → lanes, ↑/Space jump, ↓ slide, X strike, Esc pause, F11 fullscreen. Gamepad supported.
