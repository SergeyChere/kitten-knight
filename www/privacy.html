KITTEN KNIGHT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · Developer: Sergii

Kitten Knight does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

Kitten Knight is a single-player game made by an independent developer, Sergii. This policy applies to every version of the game: Android (Google Play), iPhone (App Store), desktop (Linux and other platforms) and the web version.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal or device data, and it does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

Data saved on your device

To keep your progress, the game saves a small amount of game data on your own device only:

  • best scores and your list of best runs,
  • fish collected, upgrades, unlocked heroes and the hero you picked,
  • achievements and game settings such as sound volume and graphics quality.

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party services that collect data. It does not use the advertising ID.

The app stores that distribute the game, such as Google Play and the App Store, may collect information under their own privacy policies when you download or update apps. That collection is handled by the store, not by Kitten Knight.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. On Android the app includes the standard internet permission that the app framework adds by default; the game does not use it to send or receive personal data.

Children

Kitten Knight does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, there is nothing to share or sell.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
